# Official standards and integrity sources

Rules here are [Literature/Official Standard] only within the scope stated. Re-check the live official page for submission-critical decisions.

- Nature Research Figure Guide, [Building and exporting figure panels](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/building-and-exporting-figure-panels/). Nature-specific widths, font range, panel arrangement, accessibility advice, editable text/layers, and accepted formats. Checked 2026-09-11.
- Elsevier, [Graphical abstract guidance](https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/graphical-abstract). Elsevier graphical-abstract dimensions, fonts, formats, and whitespace guidance. Scope is graphical abstracts, not all figures. Checked 2026-09-11.
- Springer Nature, [Use of an open access figure or table](https://support.springernature.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000217050-use-of-an-open-access-figure-or-table) and [Author Copyrights information](https://support.springernature.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000080095-author-copyrights-information). Reuse, credit, licence, and permission guidance.
- US Office of Research Integrity, [Guidelines for Best Practices in Image Processing](https://ori.hhs.gov/education/products/RIandImages/guidelines/list.html). Treat images as data, retain originals, document processing, avoid selective local manipulation, and use cropping/compression responsibly.

## Scope cautions

- A publisher-family page may differ from a specific journal's instructions.
- Initial submission rules may differ from accepted-artwork production rules.
- Main figures, extended data, supplements, graphical abstracts, and covers often have different formats.
- DPI applies to raster sampling; it does not by itself establish legibility, accessibility, or compliance.
- Vector preference does not mean every target accepts SVG, PDF, or EPS.
- Accessibility requires semantic alternatives and redundant encodings in addition to palette choice.
